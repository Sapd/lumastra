{{- if and (include "lumastra.routerHopOn" .) (eq (include "lumastra.routerRealIpWide" .) "true") }}

WARNING: the router adopts X-Forwarded-For from router.realIp.fromCIDRs ({{ join ", " .Values.router.realIp.fromCIDRs }}), a range
any pod in the cluster may be in (wider than a /28), and no NetworkPolicy with a narrow ingressFrom limits who reaches the router. The router-hop secret proves a request came
THROUGH the router, not that its X-Forwarded-For is honest: any pod can `curl -H 'X-Forwarded-For: <ip>' http://<release>-lumastra-router:8080/`
and choose the client address the app uses for per-IP login/auth rate limits, IP-pinned playback grants and PAT/service-token IP allowlists.
Fix: networkPolicy.enabled=true with networkPolicy.ingressFrom (your ingress controller pods; an empty selector or 0.0.0.0/0 does not count), and/or narrow
router.realIp.fromCIDRs to them (/28 or narrower). NetworkPolicy only helps if your CNI enforces it.
{{- end }}
